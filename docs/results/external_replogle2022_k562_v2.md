## Reading

A target whose own delta is clearly negative was knocked down in the external screen as it was in the bundle; a target with a small mean absolute delta had little effect there, and transferring its response adds little. The deltas are differences within the external screen (its perturbed cells minus its own controls), so batch differences between the two screens cancel only to the extent they are additive in log space.
